# Figure 3 endpoint correction: provenance and reuse boundary

The updated `handoff/fig3_data.json` replaces the historical 13-compound Roth display with the authors' existing identity-audited 12-compound aggregate result. Figure 3b now identifies the endpoint as a GI50 resistance ratio. The plotting guard reads `数据/processed/baseline_roth_external_metrics_v2_identity_safe.csv` and checks the displayed sample count, correlation, p value, identity exclusion and endpoint label. The new CSV contains six aggregate model-by-transporter rows from the authors' baseline analysis; it does not contain third-party per-molecule predictor outputs or model weights. Figure 3 uses its ABCB1/Morgan-logistic row: n = 12, Spearman rho = -0.570929, p = 0.052524. The dummy-prior rows retain blank undefined correlations rather than invented zeros.

The measured biological source is Roth JS, Guo H, Chen L, Shen M, Gbadegesin O, Robey RW, Gottesman MM, Hall MD. *Identification of antibody-drug conjugate payloads that are substrates of ATP-binding cassette drug efflux transporters*. Cancer Drug Resist. 2026;9:2. DOI: [10.20517/cdr.2025.151](https://doi.org/10.20517/cdr.2025.151). See [the original article, Table 3 and its footnote](https://www.oaepublish.com/articles/cdr.2025.151): relative resistance compares GI50 in transporter-expressing and empty-vector HEK293 cells following cytotoxicity assays. This functional resistance endpoint differs from a bidirectional permeability-based transport efflux ratio. The source reports three independent experiments. The article is [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), copyright the authors, 2026.

The EffluxScope analysis excluded the source's “Deruxtecan” record because its molecular identity could not be safely matched within this computational audit. That exclusion is an analysis decision, not a correction of the original biological publication or a conclusion that the source experiment was invalid. The twelve-compound correlation is a retrospective baseline diagnostic, not a new validation of the frozen EffluxScope screener. It has no bootstrap interval in this display and does not establish ADC-specific predictive accuracy.
