`handoff/fig2_ref_xy.npy` contains 443 pairs of frozen t-SNE coordinates calculated by the authors for the primary reference panel. These are visualization coordinates rather than external predictor outputs. The reference structures originate in the ChEMBL-derived primary pool, whose source attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 obligations remain applicable to redistributed adaptations. Preserve ChEMBL/EMBL-EBI attribution and the source-specific notices in the original study's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `REPOSITORY_THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; this coordinate supplement does not waive or relabel those obligations. The primary panel's curated substrate labels have separate documented CC BY 4.0 provenance, although labels are not contained in the two-column coordinate array.

The core study's external data and recorded aggregate comparison metrics retain their original source attribution and licensing. This supplement supplies no new molecular endpoint table, no third-party per-molecule prediction array, no model checkpoint, no full article, and no font file. The excluded AstraZeneca Figure 1 scatter arrays and Figure 4 individual comparator inputs must be obtained or regenerated under the relevant providers' terms; their absence is explicit in `README_FIGURES12.md`.

The palette files are copied unchanged from the maintained plotting sources to preserve the authors' existing figure style. They are code/style metadata, not new scientific measurements.
